The problem

Why most business owners never start.

Most business owners freeze before they open the camera app and blame it on time. Usually the real issue is that they don't know where they're going. With no plan, every post is a fresh decision and a fresh chance to quit. The Playbook gives you a plan to follow, so you're no longer asking what to say. You're just doing the next step.

What's inside

What's in the Playbook.

01

Find your foundation

Combine your professional niche with your human side so you stop sounding like everyone else in your industry. Learn the four things content actually transfers: judgment, competency, relatability, and trust.

02

Build your content pillars

Pick three to five pillars once. Every post ties back to one of them, so you always know what to talk about and your content adds up to something.

03

A production system that fits your week

How to batch, how to use a question format so you sound like yourself instead of reading a script, and how to shoot for the edit. You can make a month of content in one sitting.

04

Authority over attention

How chasing a viral post can hurt your strategy, with a real example from an HVAC and plumbing company. Plan your content like a book so you're the obvious choice when buyers are ready.

05

The Visibility Core 40

Forty questions across eight sections that get your real language onto the page. Use them to replace vague, interchangeable copy that leaves AI tools unsure who you are.

06

The 30-Day Challenge

Thirty ready-made questions, one per business day, so you never start from a blank page. The only thing you track is whether you kept the streak going.

07

The Long Game

How long consistent content takes to pay off, including the story of a fractional COO who signed three clients in the first ten days after announcing his positioning, following three years of posting.

08

Content networking

How to use podcast guest spots, your own conversations, and events to build relationships and make content at the same time, and why tagging the people you feature extends your reach.

What you'll walk away with

What you'll have when you finish.

  • A short list of content pillars, so you always know what to talk about
  • A batch workflow that takes a few hours a month instead of every day
  • 30 daily prompts you can start recording or writing today
  • A 40-question framework for making your website and profiles clear and consistent for people and AI tools
  • A realistic timeline, so you don't quit at day 30 because nothing big has happened yet

About the author

Ben G. Kaiser

Eight years ago I was a nonprofit graphic designer filming and editing laptop reviews from 4:30 to 6:30 every morning before work. That channel has since passed 52 million views and been responsible for the millions of dollars I've made over the years.

Along the way I've been a marketing manager at an agency and at a SaaS company, and a CMO at a healthcare company. In every role, the job was growing organic content: no ads, no bought placements, just something real enough that people found it on their own.

Today I run Kaiser Media Group, a full-time agency that helps companies and consultants create content that builds their brand, gets found by the people searching for solutions, and shows up when someone asks an AI who the expert is in their field.
