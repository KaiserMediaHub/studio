Where to start

Your first week.

  1. Read the Foundation section. Work out your professional side and your human side. Everything else builds on them.
  2. Pick three to five content pillars. Once they're set, you know what to talk about.
  3. Start the 30-Day Challenge. Thirty questions, one per business day. Just keep the streak going.
